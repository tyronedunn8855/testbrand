The Brand

Made To Be Noticed

lucent builds one silhouette at a time and does it properly. Drop 01 is the cropped zip and short set, cut from a heavyweight stretch fleece that holds its shape instead of sagging after a week.

Nine colorways, because a set this simple lives or dies on the color. Each one gets its own embroidery, its own zip pull, its own star at the hip.

Units are limited per colorway. When a size goes, that size is done for the drop. Nothing gets restocked, and the next drop looks nothing like this one.
